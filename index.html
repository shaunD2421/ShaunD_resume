About Me

I build and automate production infrastructure. Over the past five years at ActionHRM I've replaced manual server provisioning with SaltStack configuration management, put 40+ systems under Zabbix monitoring with custom PostgreSQL metrics, deployed pfSense firewalls and VPN access, and taken our internal environment to ACSC Essential Eight Level 2 compliance.

The work I'm proudest of is the backup pipeline. Rsync-based PostgreSQL backups wired into Zabbix feedback loops, so a failed backup raises an alert instead of being discovered during a restore. Most of what I do comes back to that idea: build the thing so it tells you when it's broken.

I am currently working alongside my manager to take ActionHRM through ISO/IEC 27001 certification, building on the Essential Eight work already in place. That means mapping controls, tightening access management and patching, and producing the evidence and documentation an audit needs.

Alongside that I design and maintain production websites for commercial clients, working in WordPress and custom HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and handling hosting, SSL, backups and updates.

I work across Linux and Windows Server, script in Bash and Python, and manage infrastructure as code. Currently completing a Bachelor of Information Technology at the University of Wollongong alongside full-time work, and working toward AWS and Terraform certification.
